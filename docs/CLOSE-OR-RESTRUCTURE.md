# Close-or-Restructure Sprint

**Feature work on the model is frozen.** The remaining risk is not another mis-coded tax rule — it is closing a 1968 village property with inherited tenants, seller paper, a formula investor exit, and a Year-5 loan that only clears under programs not yet held. This is the working checklist for the next six weeks. Use the engine to *underwrite these facts*, not to add mechanisms.

Adopted from the July 2026 peer review (`docs/REVIEW-2026-07-perplexity.md`; verdicts in ANALYSIS.md A.22).

---

## Tier 1 — Close / kill criteria (30–60 days)

Each item is a **hard gate**. If one cannot be satisfied, the deal restructures or stops.

### 1.1 Phase-2 takeout package — the binding constraint
- [ ] Run the subject address through USDA's property eligibility map and **print the result** (population test favours Yellow Springs at ~3,700 — under the 10,000 threshold, so MSA membership does not reach it; A.19.3)
- [ ] List of USDA-approved § 538 lenders serving Ohio; contact at least three
- [ ] Indicative § 538 term sheet: rate, term, amortisation, LTV, DSCR, reserve and replacement-reserve requirements, borrower-type rules (nonprofit vs for-profit leverage differs), rehab hard-cost minimums
- [ ] Parallel indication from a CDFI and from NCB or another co-op blanket lender — **confirm their LTV/DSCR overlays and whether a co-op/CLT stack is even eligible**
- [ ] Confirm whether the lender's appraiser will use restricted income or market comps (this decides the whole feasibility test — A.21.2)

**Gate:** without a real term sheet, gap closure is a hypothesis, not a plan.

### 1.2 Tax counsel memo (short, but before capital is solicited)
- [ ] One-page **entity stack**: who holds title in Years 0–5, and how the syndicate becomes a tenant LEC without a taxable transfer, transfer tax, or broken installment-sale characterisation
- [ ] Investor interest as **equity not debt** (§ 385 factors) — the single point of failure for the tax thesis
- [ ] § 704(b) substantial economic effect for allocating depreciation to investors
- [ ] § 216 / Subchapter T path if the operating entity is a housing co-op during the hold
- [ ] Securities: Reg D on the investor raise **and** on member shares; Ohio blue sky
- [ ] Grant paper characterisation if any grant is taken (deferred loan vs income — § 61/§ 118 drives whether basis survives)
- [ ] **Written opinion or issues list as a closing condition**, not a post-close nice-to-have

### 1.3 Building truth set — replace model defaults that drive rent floors
- [ ] 12 months of **actuals**: property tax, insurance, water/sewer/trash, repairs, utilities
- [ ] Village **apartment-class** water/sewer rate schedule (the model's $1,500/unit/yr is a placeholder, not an estimate)
- [ ] Rough **Property Condition Assessment**: roof, boilers, electric, plumbing, envelope
- [ ] Lead / asbestos / accessibility capital exposure
- [ ] Habitational insurance **indication** — bindability, not just trend rates
- [ ] Rent roll **with per-unit security deposit balances** (deposits transfer at closing and can seed member shares — A.18 discussion)

### 1.4 Appraisal scopes — commissioned separately, no subtraction games
- [ ] **Land parcel** (for the § 170 gift) — qualified appraiser, Form 8283 rules
- [ ] **Leasehold improvements** as the co-op acquires them
- [ ] As-is fee simple vs stabilized restricted value
- [ ] **Consistency memo**: as-is ~$1.25M, restricted stabilized value, any cash offer, land residual — one narrative, not four convenient numbers
- [ ] CLT donee acknowledgment; ground-lease form that does not depress land FMV into absurdity

### 1.5 Seller decision package (one page for Paul)
- [ ] Cash at closing: cash sale vs note (the note's answer is deliberately much smaller — A.19.5)
- [ ] Philanthropy cost of the land gift, stated as philanthropy
- [ ] Balloon-year tax spike and whether § 170 capacity still covers it (A.20.2)
- [ ] Credit risk on the co-op; AFR floor on the note
- [ ] **What happens on default once the land is already at the CLT** — this is the question he will ask and it needs an answer in writing
- [ ] Estate flag: the note is IRD under § 691 (no step-up); consider bequeathing any remaining balance to the CLT
- [ ] Do **not** pledge the note as collateral — § 453A(d) deems it a payment and accelerates the deferred gain

## Tier 2 — Structure the capital and the co-op

### 2.1 Named capital path
- [ ] Identify **three people who would actually write the check**. Minimum check size.
- [ ] REPS or passive? If only passive capital exists, the base case is the passive IRR and bonus-depreciation reliance must shrink.
- [ ] Does a 7% preferred clear with those investors, or is the pricing fiction?
- [ ] Equity indicia to add: thin promote or residual share (e.g. 5–10% of refinance surplus above a hurdle), board seat, major-decision votes
- [ ] Strike any language guaranteeing return of capital on a date certain

### 2.2 Member conversion plan
- [ ] Legal rights and status of **non-buying households** — the dual-class occupancy question, drafted rather than assumed
- [ ] Share assistance fund: revolving, ~$8,000 seed, deposit credit applied first
- [ ] Governance timeline, board training, **D&O insurance** for a new 25-unit LEC
- [ ] No-pressure rule in writing; no screening of sitting tenants
- [ ] Collections and bad-debt plan through conversion (defaults now carry 6% vacancy for this reason)

### 2.3 Documents
- [ ] Ground lease: stewardship rent at the floor, term exceeding any mortgage, solvency rent override
- [ ] Management agreement **separate** from the ground lease (UBIT)
- [ ] Surplus / shadow-equity policy drafted non-redeemable or hard-capped so it does not surprise a refinance lender or disturb § 216

## Tier 3 — Model work, only after Tier 1 is underway

- [ ] Freeze a **base / downside / upside** preset set (no grants; § 538 only; grants + § 538) and stop adding mechanisms
- [ ] Named **conversion-year stress** scenario (vacancy 6–8%, reserves $550+, CapEx $12–15k/door) — not a buried sensitivity
- [ ] If anything new is coded: takeout **term-sheet fields** and a debt-equity opinion checklist. Not more MACRS nuance.

### Explicitly stopped

- Finer escalator choreography
- Donation-size optimisers beyond what exists
- IRR cosmetics without a term sheet

## Standing honesty rules for any deck or member meeting

1. **Lead with the takeout gap, DSCR and takeout path** — not with Phase-1 rent victories that are financed by Year-5 fragility.
2. **Grants are a sensitivity, not a plan.** "Fully chiselled" ladders are upside only; the model defaults them to $0 at close for that reason.
3. **The donation optimiser sizes tax capacity, not land value.** Only the appraisal sets value.
4. **Member shares are not primarily a rent lever** — ~$40k moves rent about $10/unit/month. Say so every time.
5. **REPS is a fact about a named investor, not a setting.** Quote the passive IRR unless someone specific qualifies.
6. **Self-management at 25 units is not Greenmont at 500.** Do not stack self-management and deep grants in the same "success" column as the base case.
